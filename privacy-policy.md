# Privacy Policy for Pothole AI

Pothole AI uses the device camera and location services to detect and map road damage such as potholes.

Information collected:
- Camera input for real-time pothole detection
- GPS location for mapping detected potholes

Data usage:
- Detection processing is performed on-device using Core ML.
- Location data is only used to associate pothole detections with map coordinates.
- No personal information is sold or shared with third parties.
- The app does not use advertising or tracking technologies.

Pothole AI does not require user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information.
